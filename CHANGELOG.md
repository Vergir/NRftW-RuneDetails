# Changelog

## 1.1.0

* Scream and the four Novas show their area radius (Scream: 6m; Novas: 3–6m by charge).
* Detailed mode: Scream says whether its knockdown hits allies (it does).
* The settings row shares its code with the other mods of the series.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

* Brief mode: one grey note at the end of every rune text: damage as a multiple of weapon damage (per hit, areas,
  waves, projectiles, charge levels), heals, buffs, channel drain, kicks' live damage, throws' share of base enemy HP,
  traps and mines.
* Detailed mode: cost (as cast and per second for channels), damage with its own element, poise damage, knockback,
  knockdown, stagger, cast time, lockout (and after a combo), invulnerability windows, hyper armor, efficiency, and
  whether area damage hits allies.
* Works on item tooltips, vendors, utility slots and the Runes menu; type and cost are shown once on every screen.
* Off / Brief / Detailed in Options > Gameplay.
